ENUMS:
  transaction_type = income | expense | transfer

TABLES:
  habits(id, name, description, frequency, target_min, target_max, recording_required, current_streak, longest_streak)
  habit_logs(habit_id, log_date, value)
  projects(id, parent_id, name, description, due_at, started_at, finished_at)
  tasks(id, project_id, name, description, due_at, started_at, finished_at, task_type, recurrence, priority)
  todos(id, task_id, name, is_done)
  time_entries(id, task_id, started_at, finished_at, comment)
  accounts(id, name, total, created_at)
  categories(id, name, parent_id, type, created_at)
  transactions(id, type, amount, account_id, to_account_id, category_id, description, occurred_at, created_at)
  plan_blocks(id, plan_date, started_at, ended_at, task_id, label, note)
  concello_marks(id, name, visited_on, description, created_at)
  weed_varieties(id, name, scent, flavor, power, quality, score, price, comments, judge)

RULES:
  - Motor PostgreSQL: genera SELECT válido para Postgres, usando solo las tablas/columnas de arriba.
  - id y created_at los asigna el servidor. accounts.total lo mantiene un trigger: se puede leer, nunca escribir.
  - transactions.amount > 0. transactions.category.type debe coincidir con transactions.type.
  - transfer: to_account_id no nulo y distinto de account_id. income/expense: to_account_id nulo.
  - Un hábito "hecho" un día = hay fila en habit_logs con ese log_date. frequency = daily|weekly|monthly.
  - tasks sin finished_at estan pendientes. task_type = standard|continuous|recurring; priority 1..5.
  - Los límites de "mes"/"hoy" son en horario local Europe/Madrid.
